I set technical direction and change live systems without stopping them, working as part of your team rather than around it.

At IBM I held architecture authority across a partner platform serving 200k users. Before that I led development of My Sales Activity, which cut the lead time on a new incentive from at least six months to a couple of days.

Where there is a legacy system in the way, I would rather replace it piece by piece while it stays live than plan a cutover. At IBM I led the development that let three successive migrations run incrementally: an abstraction that connects to both the outgoing and the incoming system is what makes a migration survivable.
