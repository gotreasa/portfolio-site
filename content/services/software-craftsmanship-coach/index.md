I coach development teams towards software craftsmanship: test-driven development, clean code, pair programming and continuous delivery, worked on your own codebase rather than on exercises.

The standard I coach to is graded and assessed on delivered work rather than on self-report, so a team can tell whether the practice has actually taken hold. I led that work across 1,000 developers on three continents at IBM, and I was instructor and embedded coach on it inside a Fortune 100 insurer and at Nederlandse Spoorwegen, the Dutch national railway.

Most of the difficulty is not technical. Practice changes that leadership has not internalised do not survive the first delivery deadline, so I coach the managers and product people alongside the engineers.
